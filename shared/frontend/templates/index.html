Built from focused services

Each service owns one part of the picture, so the system stays easy to reason about as it grows.

🪴

Virtual Garden

The source of truth for your garden — beds, plants, plantings, and every watering, fertilising, and observation event.

📚

Plant Almanac

General plant knowledge — sunlight, soil, water, and seasonal needs, queryable by other services and AI assistants via MCP.

🩺

Health Monitoring

Combines your garden's actual state with almanac knowledge to flag possible disease, pests, and watering problems early.

🗓️

Scheduler

Turns garden changes into future tasks — watering, fertilising, pruning, harvest — and keeps them up to date as things change.

🔔

Notifications

Timely alerts for watering, frost, heat, and harvest windows — driven by events, not hardcoded gardening logic.

🤖

AI Input Layer

Turns natural language and photos into structured garden observations, so any input method leads to the same clean data.
