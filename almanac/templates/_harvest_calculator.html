How this is calculated

Your weekly target is grams per person × people. The first batch covers a full harvest window. Later batches share the target across overlapping harvests, rounded up to whole plants.

Yield is averaged across the harvest window. Seasonal planting limits, weather and crop losses can affect supply. Adjust the inputs to match your garden.
